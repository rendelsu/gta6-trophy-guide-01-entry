      Settings & data

      Spoiler-safe mode

      When on, story trophies you have not earned yet show as “??? — hidden story trophy” instead of their name and description. Earned trophies always show in full.

      Save & share your progress

      Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) as you tick trophies. Export a JSON backup, or import one to restore it on another device.

      Where the data comes from

      Official names, descriptions and grades: the PlayStation list as published on Exophase, cross-checked against PSNProfiles (frozen 18 Nov 2026, verified live 19 Nov 2026 — the two agree on all 55). PSN progress counts (e.g. 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts) come from PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites its sources beside it. No Rockstar logo or artwork is used; the look is our own. The app works fully offline — it fetches nothing.
